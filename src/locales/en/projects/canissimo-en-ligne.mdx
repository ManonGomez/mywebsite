## The brief

Create an online learning platform for dog training and activities, easy for learners to use and for the client to manage.

## My contribution

I supported the project from understanding requirements through to technical implementation: organising training, structuring courses and content, designing the learner journey and configuring the LMS environment.

I adapted and customised WordPress and LearnDash to meet the project's functional requirements.

I also provided onboarding so the client could manage the platform independently.
